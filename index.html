Madeline Heller is an art historian, writer, and emerging curator based in New York. She holds an MA from The Courtauld Institute of Art and has worked at Christie’s, London, where she developed her understanding of how art acquires value, moves through institutions, and enters the historical record. She is currently an associate for a private estate sales and liquidation company and is developing Flesch, an independent gallery program and arts publication based in New York.
