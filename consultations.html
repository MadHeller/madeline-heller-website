How It Works

  1. Choose a session and a time with the “Book & pay” button. Sessions are available on Wednesdays and Sundays, at least two days ahead.
  2. Pay securely when you book. You’ll receive a confirmation email with a video-call link.
  3. We meet by video. For assessments, please send clear photographs beforehand: front, back, any signature or labels, and measurements.

Cancel at least one day before your session for a full refund; you can also reschedule from your confirmation email. Consultations offer professional opinion and guidance only. They are not formal appraisals, authentication, or financial, legal or investment advice.
