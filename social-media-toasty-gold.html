Umbrella Girl & Umbrella Boy: Hummel Figurines

☔️ Weathering the storm, one Hummel at a time.

Meet the newest (well, technically the oldest-looking) additions to our collection: Umbrella Girl and Umbrella Boy, caught mid-windstorm and holding onto their umbrellas for dear life. There’s something so charming about the way Goebel’s sculptors captured motion in porcelain. You see it in the wind-blown hair, the flapping umbrella canopies, and the scrunched-up little expressions of “well, this is happening.”

These two have been a joy to add to the shelf. Every Hummel has its own tiny drama, and this one is basically a two-part sitcom about British weather.

🧵 Swipe for a closer look at the hand-painted details seen in their freckles, the mismatched socks, and the sheer commitment to a bad hair day. And don’t worry! We have an enormous collection of Hummel figures for you to see. Go to our bio to click on our link.
