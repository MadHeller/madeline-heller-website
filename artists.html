About Flesch

Flesch is an independent gallery program based in New York, founded by art historian and curator Madeline Heller. It works with emerging artists and with collectors, placing works with people who will live with them. In The Flesch, a magazine of art criticism and market analysis, is coming soon.

The name Flesch comes from my great-great-grandmother, Fanny Flesch, shown here with my grandmother on her wedding day. Flesch is a way of carrying her name forward.

What I’m Looking For

Emerging and early-career artists whose work rewards a second look. All media are welcome, with a particular interest in painting and works on paper.

What Flesch Offers

Depending on the work and the fit, this can include:

  • Placement with collectors

    Introducing your work to collectors I know and helping place pieces with them.

  • Online features

    Featuring your work on this website and on Flesch’s Instagram and Threads, @flesch_inc.

  • Exhibitions & pop-ups

    Including your work in shows, pop-ups and viewing events.

  • Writing about your work

    Reviews and features in In The Flesch, the Flesch magazine of art criticism (coming soon).
